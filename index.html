generative_search_and_answer_engines

GEO and AEO specialization

I help brands appear in search results written by language models and in answer boxes. The work covers entity clarity, passage-level answers, schema and citation-ready content.

  • Generative Engine Optimization: structure content so language-model search tools cite and summarize it correctly.
  • Answer Engine Optimization: format direct answers, definitions, steps and comparisons for instant answer surfaces.
  • Citation readiness: original data, named sources, dates, author details and clear claims.
  • Entity consistency: same brand name, description and links across the site and external profiles.
  • Answer extraction: question-led headings, short answer blocks, tables and ordered lists.
  • Schema for answers: FAQPage, HowTo, Article, Person, Organization and BreadcrumbList.
  • llms.txt and llms-full.txt: machine-readable summaries for language-model tools.
  • Measurement: share of voice in answer boxes, citation frequency, prompt coverage and click-through from generative results.

glossary and faq

Key definitions

Glossary of SEO, GEO and AEO terms used on this page
TermDefinition
GEO Generative Engine Optimization. Work that helps language-model search tools understand, summarize and cite a page.
AEO Answer Engine Optimization. Work that formats content as direct answers for answer boxes and chat assistants.
llms.txt A plain text file that gives language-model tools a short summary of a site and its key pages.
Answer extraction Writing short, factual passages that can be lifted into an answer box or chat response.
Entity optimization Keeping the brand name, description and links consistent so search systems know exactly who you are.
Citation readiness Using original data, named sources, dates and clear claims so a page is safe to quote.
Core Web Vitals Google performance metrics: LCP, INP and CLS. Each is a confirmed ranking signal.
Crawl Budget The number of URLs a search engine is willing to crawl on your site within a given timeframe.
ASO App Store Optimization. The mobile-app equivalent of SEO for App Store and Play Store listings.

Q: What makes your GEO and AEO approach different?
I optimise directly for language-model search tools using schema markup, direct-answer blocks, glossary definitions and structured question-and-answer sections. This approach increased one client's answer visibility by 35 percent in three months.
Q: How do you measure visibility in generative search?
I track a fixed set of prompts per topic, then record whether the brand is cited, linked or summarized. I also measure answer box presence, citation frequency, referral traffic and assisted conversions from language-model search results.
Q: What is llms.txt and do I need it?
llms.txt is a plain text file that gives language-model tools a short summary of your site and its most important pages. It helps them understand your brand faster. It is useful for any site that wants accurate citations and summaries.
Q: How do you get cited by language-model search tools?
I use original data, named sources, clear dates, author details, short answer passages, question-led headings and schema markup. I also keep brand name, description and links consistent across the site and external profiles.
Q: What is the difference between GEO and AEO?
GEO focuses on how language-model search tools understand, summarize and cite a page. AEO focuses on formatting direct answers for answer boxes, chat assistants and instant answer surfaces. They overlap, but GEO is broader and AEO is more about answer formatting.
Q: How long does GEO take to show results?
Technical and schema fixes can appear in weeks. Citation and answer box gains usually take two to four months, depending on competition, content depth and how often language-model tools refresh their sources.
Q: Do you optimise for Google AI Overviews?
Yes. AI Overviews use similar signals to answer boxes and language-model search. I focus on passage-level clarity, entity consistency, schema and citation-worthy content that can be lifted into an overview.
